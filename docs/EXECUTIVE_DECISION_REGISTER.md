## Decision rule

The dashboard is a decision layer, not a claim that the observed historical differences are causal.

**Scale:** incremental KPI lift + acceptable economics and guardrails.

**Iterate:** positive but weak, heterogeneous, or operationally improvable effect.

**Stop:** no incremental value or unfavorable economics / guardrails.
